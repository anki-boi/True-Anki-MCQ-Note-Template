MCQ Cloze: rendering samples

Recall first: every blank starts as [...]. A blank with choices behind it is orange and glows softly; tap it to open its choices. A plain blue blank has none, exactly like Anki's own cloze. Flip without tapping anything and the card works as a normal recall card.

A small script copies the HTML Anki produces for cloze cards (span.cloze[data-cloze] on the front, span.cloze-inactive for other blanks), so these run in any browser and the adapter reads them exactly as it would in Anki.

Keys go to the card with the blue outline (click a card to move it): Tab or arrows open the next blank, 1 to 9 pick, Esc closes.
